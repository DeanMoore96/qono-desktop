Before Qono starts

Qono does not load, read the screen, or use the microphone until you agree.

Screen reading

When you share, Qono can see only the window or screen you pick. It does not read other windows or screens on its own.

Actions on screen

Qono can take actions inside the Qono page, and only after you approve each action. It does not take system-wide control of your mouse or keyboard.

Voice and microphone

Voice commands use your microphone when you turn them on. Qono uses OpenAI API voices. The voice is an AI-generated voice, not a human. Qono does not claim that OpenAI endorses this app.

Data, and where it goes

  • Screen frames and text from what you share are sent to the AI model you connect.
  • Qono's own service runs on its servers on Base44.
  • ChatGPT, Claude, and any other model connect only if you use that model's connect button in the app. You can disconnect a model from inside Qono.

Nothing above starts until you choose Agree.
